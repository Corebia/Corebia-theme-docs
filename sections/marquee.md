# Marquee

**Marquee** scrolls a line of short text or logos continuously across the page. It works as a band of brand statements between sections, or as a strip of press and partner logos.

It can't be placed in the header or footer groups. Two presets are available when you add it: **Marquee**, with three text items, and **Logo marquee**, with five logos at slow speed.

## Settings

- **Speed.** **Slow**, **Medium** (default) or **Fast**.
- **Space between items.** **Compact**, **Standard** (default) or **Spacious**.
- **Direction.** **Toward the reading start** (default) or **Toward the reading end**. On a left-to-right language the first moves the items to the left; on a right-to-left language it moves them to the right.
- **Pause on hover.** Stops the motion while the pointer rests on it. Default: on.
- **Color scheme.** Default: scheme-1.

### Spacing

- **Top padding** / **Bottom padding.** Range: 0 to 300 px in 5 px steps. Default: 40 px each.

## Blocks

Mix the two block types in any order.

### Text

- **Text.** One line of text, with bold, italic and links available. Default: `Add your text`.

### Logo

- **Logo.** The logo image. Its alt text, set in **Content > Files**, is what names the logo for screen readers, so fill it in.
- **Link.** Optional. Makes the logo a link.

## Motion and accessibility

A pause button sits beside the marquee, so shoppers can always stop it. For shoppers who ask their device for reduced motion, nothing moves: the items show once, wrapped onto as many lines as they need.

## Tips

- **Keep each text item to a few words.** A marquee is read in passing. A sentence scrolls away before anyone finishes it.
- **Use logos of a similar weight.** A mix of heavy and delicate marks makes the strip look uneven even at the same size. Transparent PNG or SVG files on the section's background look cleanest.
- **Slow suits logos, Medium suits text.** Logos need a moment to be recognized; short words can move faster.
