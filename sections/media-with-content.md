# Media with content

**Media with content** puts an image or a video on one side and your own content on the other. The content side is built from [theme blocks](../theme-blocks/), so it can hold a heading, text and a button, or anything else the theme blocks offer, plus app blocks.

It can't be placed in the header or footer groups. Three presets are available when you add it:

- **Image with text.** An image with a heading, text and a button.
- **Split hero.** A full-screen image with a heading and a button, and no padding, for opening a page.
- **Video with text.** A video with a heading, text and a button.

## Settings

- **Media.** **Image** (default) or **Video**.
- **Image.** Shown when Media is set to Image. The crop follows the focal point set on the image in the image picker. Without an image, a placeholder illustration shows.
- **Video.** Shown when Media is set to Video. Nothing loads until a shopper presses play; the video's preview image shows until then.
- **Media position.** **Left** (default) or **Right**. On mobile the media always sits above the content.
- **Media width.** **Half** (default) splits the row evenly; **Wide** gives the media three fifths of it.
- **Content alignment.** Where the content sits against the media. **Top**, **Center** (default) or **Bottom**.
- **Height.** **Fit the media** (default) sizes the section to the image or video; **Full screen** makes it fill the window.
- **Color scheme.** Default: scheme-1.

### Spacing

- **Top padding** / **Bottom padding.** Range: 0 to 300 px in 5 px steps. Default: 60 px each.

## Blocks

The content side accepts every [theme block](../theme-blocks/) (heading, text, button, group, image, icon, video, countdown and the rest) and app blocks. They stack in the order you arrange them.

## Tips

- **Alternate the media position down a page.** Two or three of these sections stacked with the media left, then right, then left read as one designed page rather than a row of repeats.
- **Use Wide when the picture carries the message.** Half suits a paragraph of real copy; Wide suits a strong photograph with a short line beside it.
- **Set a focal point on the image.** On a tall row the image is cropped, and the focal point decides what survives the crop.
- **Pick a video with a telling preview image.** The preview is what most shoppers see, since the video only plays when pressed.
