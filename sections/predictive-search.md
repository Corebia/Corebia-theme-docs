# Predictive search

**Predictive search** is the results panel that drops down as a shopper types in the header's search field. This section has no place on a page of its own. It exists so that you can configure what those live results contain.

You'll find it in the theme editor under the header group, alongside the [Header](../header/).

## Settings

- **Show vendor.** Shows the product's vendor on each result. Default: off.
- **Show price.** Default: on.
- **Show collections.** Includes matching collections in the results. Default: on.
- **Show pages.** Includes matching pages. Default: on.
- **Show articles.** Includes matching blog articles. Default: on.
- **Product results limit.** How many products the panel shows. Range: 2 to 10. Default: 4.

## What it searches

Predictive search is provided by Shopify and queries products, collections, pages and articles that are published to the Online Store sales channel. It matches on titles, product types, vendors, tags and variant data.

Results are live: they update as the shopper types, and a shopper who presses Enter goes through to the full [Search page](../../templates/search-page/), which has filters and sorting that the dropdown panel does not.

## Tips

- **Keep the product limit low.** Four results fit on a phone without the panel taking the whole screen. Ten turns a quick lookup into a scroll.
- **Turn off what you don't have.** A store with no blog gains nothing from **Show articles** except an occasional irrelevant result.
- **Vendor is for multi-brand stores.** If everything in your catalog is your own label, the vendor line repeats your own name on every row.
- **Improve results in Shopify, not here.** What ranks where is Shopify's, and it responds to product titles, tags and the Search & Discovery app's synonyms, not to settings in the theme.
