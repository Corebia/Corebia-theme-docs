# Collection tabs

**Collection tabs** puts up to four collections behind tabs in one section, for example New in, Best sellers and Sale. Each tab shows a grid of that collection's products, so a shopper can switch between them without leaving the page.

It can't be placed in the header or footer groups.

## Settings

- **Heading.** Default: `Shop by collection`.

### Product cards

- **Image ratio.** **Adapt to image**, **Portrait (3:4)**, **Square (1:1)** (default) or **Landscape (4:3)**.
- **Show vendor.** Default: off.
- **Show second image on hover.** Default: on.
- **Color scheme.** Default: scheme-1.

The rest of the card, such as quick add, swatches and badges, follows [Product cards](../../theme-settings/product-cards/) in theme settings.

### Spacing

- **Top padding** / **Bottom padding.** Range: 0 to 300 px in 5 px steps. Default: 60 px each.

## Blocks

Up to **four** Collection tab blocks. The section starts with three, labeled `New in`, `Best sellers` and `Sale`, waiting for their collections.

- **Collection.** The collection this tab shows. Until one is chosen, the tab shows placeholder cards.
- **Tab label.** Leave empty to use the collection's title.
- **Products.** How many of the collection's products the tab shows. Range: 1 to 8. Default: 4.
- **Columns on desktop.** Range: 2 to 4. Default: 4.
- **Show "View all" link.** A link to the full collection under the grid. Default: on.

## How the tabs behave

- With two or more tabs, shoppers switch between them with a click, or with the arrow keys once a tab has focus.
- With a single tab there is nothing to switch, so the section shows that collection's heading and grid without a tab row.
- With no tabs the section shows nothing on your storefront, and a reminder to add one in the theme editor.

## Tips

- **Keep tab labels to one or two words.** They sit side by side on a phone, and long labels push the last tab out of view.
- **Match the product count to the columns.** Four products in four columns, or eight in four, fills whole rows. Six in four leaves a gap.
- **Give each tab a different job.** New, best-selling and on-sale products answer different questions. Three tabs of similar collections look like one collection split in three.
