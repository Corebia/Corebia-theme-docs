# Announcement bar

The **Announcement bar** is a thin top bar that appears above the header on every page. Use it for time-sensitive messages: free shipping thresholds, sale announcements, holiday hours.

It is part of the header section group, so it can be added, hidden, or reordered from the **Header** section group inside the theme editor.

## Settings

- **Text** — The message shown in the bar. Default: `Welcome to our store`.
- **Link** — Optional URL the entire bar links to. Leave blank for a non-clickable bar.
- **Color scheme** — The color scheme applied to the bar. Default: scheme-3 (dark).

## Tips

- **Keep it short.** Long copy wraps awkwardly on mobile. Aim for under 60 characters.
- **One announcement at a time.** Add multiple announcement bar sections only if you have a clear reason; layered bars push the page content down and feel pushy.
- **Use a contrasting color scheme** so the bar reads as separate from the header.
- **Link to the relevant page.** If the bar promises free shipping, link it to your shipping policy or a sale collection.

## How to add or remove

1. Open the theme editor.
2. In the left sidebar, scroll to **Header** under the page section group.
3. Click **Add section** at the top of the header group, then choose **Announcement bar**.
4. To remove, click the section and use the **...** menu > **Remove section**.
