# Complete the look

**Complete the look** sells an outfit or a set in one click. It lists two to six products you choose, each with a checkbox and, where the product has variants, a menu to pick one. A running total and a single button add every ticked product to the cart together. An optional look image sits beside the list.

It can't be placed in the header or footer groups.

## Settings

- **Look image.** Optional. A photograph of the whole look, shown beside the list.
- **Heading.** Default: `Complete the look`.
- **Products.** Two to six products. The look shows once two of them are available.
- **Color scheme.** Default: scheme-1.

### Spacing

- **Top padding** / **Bottom padding.** Range: 0 to 300 px in 5 px steps. Default: 60 px each.

## How it behaves

- Every available product starts ticked. The shopper unticks what they already own, and the total and the button label (`Add 3 items to cart`) follow along.
- A product with more than one variant gets a menu of its variants. Choosing one updates that row's price and the total. Sold-out variants are listed but can't be chosen.
- A sold-out product still shows, marked **Sold out**, but it can't be ticked.
- With fewer than two available products the section shows nothing on your storefront. In the theme editor it shows a note asking for at least two.

## Tips

- **Photograph the look as a whole.** The image is what sells the set; the list is how it gets bought. A flat lay or a single styled shot works better than a product photo of one of the pieces.
- **Three or four products is the sweet spot.** Two barely reads as a look, and six makes a long list with a long total.
- **Lead with the anchor piece.** Products are listed in the order you pick them, so put the one the look is built around first.
- **Watch availability.** When a piece sells out the look quietly shrinks, and below two available products it disappears. Swap sold-out pieces for something in stock.
