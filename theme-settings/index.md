# Theme settings

Theme settings apply globally, across every page of your storefront. To open them, go to **Online Store > Themes > Customize** on Pave, then click **Theme settings** at the bottom of the left sidebar.

They are the settings worth getting right first: colors, type and the product card shape it in every section at once, so setting them before you build pages saves revisiting each one.

- [Colors](colors/): Color schemes, used by every section.
- [Typography](typography/): Heading and body fonts, and the two size scales.
- [Social media](social-media/): Profile URLs behind the footer icons.
- [Logo](logo/): Logo image and width.
- [Favicon](favicon/): Browser icon and the fallback social share image.
- [Cart settings](cart-settings/): The discount code field.
- [Product cards](product-cards/): Image ratio, quick add, card swatches and the new badge.
- [Discount display](discount-display/): How sale badges are written and colored.
- [Variant picker](variant-picker/): Swatch style and how out-of-stock variants are shown.

Settings that belong to one section are documented under [Sections](../sections/); settings that belong to one template are under [Templates](../templates/).
