# Theme settings

Theme settings apply globally across every page of your storefront. To open them, go to **Online Store > Themes > Customize** on Pave, then click **Theme settings** at the bottom of the left sidebar.

Pave's theme settings are organized into the following categories. Each one is documented on its own page below.

- [Colors](colors/) — Color schemes used across all sections.
- [Typography](typography/) — Heading and body fonts, scaling.
- [Social media](social-media/) — Profile URLs for footer icons.
- [Logo](logo/) — Shop logo image and width.
- [Favicon](favicon/) — Browser favicon and social share image.
- [Cart settings](cart-settings/) — Discount code field toggle.
- [Product cards](product-cards/) — Product image ratio used everywhere products are listed.

Settings inside individual sections are documented under [Sections](../sections/header/).
