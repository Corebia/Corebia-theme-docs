# Layout

How wide the content of the storefront is allowed to run on large screens.

## Settings

- **Page width.** The maximum width of page content. **Narrow** (1200 px), **Normal** (default, 1440 px) or **Full**, which lets content run to the edges of the window.

On screens narrower than the chosen width, nothing changes: the content already fills the screen. The setting only shows on large desktop displays.

Full-bleed elements, such as the Hero banner image, span the window whatever you choose. The setting governs the text, grids and product layouts inside them.

## Tips

- **Narrow suits text-led stores.** Shorter lines are easier to read, and a small catalog looks more considered in a tighter frame.
- **Full suits image-led grids.** Collections with a lot of photography use the extra room well. Long paragraphs don't; at full width on a large monitor a line of text becomes hard to follow.
- **Check on a large monitor.** On a laptop screen the three options can look identical. The difference appears at 1440 px and wider.
