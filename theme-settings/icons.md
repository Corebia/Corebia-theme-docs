# Icons

The weight of the line icons used throughout the theme: the menu, search, cart and account icons in the header, arrows, close buttons and the chevrons in menus and selects.

## Settings

- **Stroke.** **Thin**, **Default** (default) or **Heavy**.

## Tips

- **Match the stroke to the type.** A light body font sits well with **Thin** icons; a heavy heading font with **Heavy** ones. Icons drawn heavier than the text around them look pasted in.
- **Thin icons need contrast.** Icons take their color from the section's color scheme, and a thin line fades faster than text does on a low-quality screen. If you choose Thin, check the header and the cart drawer on a phone.
