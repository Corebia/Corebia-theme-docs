# Buttons

The shape and lettering of every button in the store. Pave has two kinds: the **primary** button, filled, for the main action on a page such as **Add to cart**; and the **secondary** button, outlined, for everything alongside it. Their colors come from the section's color scheme, under [Colors](../colors/).

## Settings

### Primary button

- **Border thickness.** Range: 0 to 4 px. Default: 0 px.
- **Corner radius.** Range: 0 to 100 px. Default: 100 px, a full pill.
- **Font.** **Body** (default), **Accent** or **Heading**. The fonts themselves are set under [Typography](../typography/).
- **Text case.** **Default** (default) or **Uppercase**.

### Secondary button

- **Border thickness.** Range: 0 to 4 px. Default: 1 px.
- **Corner radius.** Range: 0 to 100 px. Default: 100 px.
- **Font.** **Body** (default), **Accent** or **Heading**.
- **Text case.** **Default** (default) or **Uppercase**.

### Pills

- **Corner radius.** Used for active filter chips and variant pills. Range: 0 to 40 px. Default: 40 px.

## Tips

- **Keep the two radii the same.** A pill-shaped primary button next to a square secondary one looks like two different design systems. Change both together.
- **Give the secondary button a border.** At 0 px an outlined button has no outline, and it reads as plain text that happens to be clickable.
- **Match the pills to the buttons.** If you square off the buttons, bring **Pills** down too, so the variant picker and filters follow the same shape.
- **Uppercase wants a little restraint.** It suits short labels like `Shop` or `Add to cart`. With the heading font at a heavy weight it can start to shout.
