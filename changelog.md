# Changelog

Pave version history, newest first.

## 1.0.0

The first release of the theme.

Pave is built for stores that lead with photography: a spacious, editorial storefront where the images carry the page and the interface stays out of the way. Color, type, buttons and product card styling are set once in theme settings and apply across every template, so a store can be rebranded without opening a section.

### Added

- **A design system in theme settings.** Page width, per-level heading fonts and sizes, button, field, popover and badge shapes, icon stroke weight and product card style, all set once for the whole store.
- **A color scheme system** covering backgrounds, text, borders, buttons, form fields and feedback states, applied per section, with three schemes set up out of the box and an optional dark mode that follows the visitor's device.
- **A header with two desktop styles**: an inline bar with the logo, top-level links, search and the cart, or the menu button alone. Mega menus with text columns, collection images or product images and a promotion tile; a side panel with three levels of nesting; a sticky header, a back-to-top button and an optional mobile bottom bar.
- **Four variant picker styles.** Three swatch sizes and a dropdown, chosen once in theme settings, with color options as swatches, size options as buttons and every other option as a menu, or one style for every option.
- **Home page sections**: Hero banner, New arrivals, Brand message, Collection list, Brand image and Journal, with a Newsletter section above the footer on every page and an optional Newsletter popup, off by default.
- **Content sections**: Slideshow, Media with content, Rich text with image, Marquee, Collage, Scrollytelling with hotspots, a flexible Section built from theme blocks, Customer reviews, FAQ, Contact form and Store locations.
- **Theme blocks** for building your own layouts: heading, text, button, image, video, icon, jumbo text, countdown, comparison slider, spacer, group, divider and custom Liquid.
- **Merchandising sections**: Featured product with full app-block support, Product recommendations in related or complementary mode, Recently viewed, Collection links, Collection tabs, Shop by colour, Shoppable image, Shoppable videos, Complete the look and Drop, a launch page with a countdown and a waitlist.
- **Custom Liquid** as both a section and a block across the main sections.
- **Product page** with a configurable gallery (Stacked, 2 columns, Thumbnails, Thumbnail slideshow) that can run to the screen edge, open images full screen for zoom and show only the selected variant's media. Sticky product information, and blocks for vendor, heading, price, text, SKU, inventory status with a low-stock bar and a pre-order message, variant picker, buy buttons, personalization, delivery estimate, description, collapsible tabs, details, pop-up, ask a question, share, product rating, icon with text, previous and next product, and custom Liquid.
- **Quantity rules, volume pricing and combined listings** on the product page, and a size the shopper picked remembered across products, with their consent.
- **Size guide** from a page, per product via metafield, and optionally on every option.
- **Rich product media** including video, external video and 3D models, with image focal points for desktop and mobile.
- **Product cards** with quick add, a quick view popup, an image carousel, video on hover, a size selector, color swatches, star ratings, tag badges and a new badge with a configurable window.
- **Discount display settings** governing badge wording, minimum threshold, colors, and promotion labels driven by product metafields.
- **Collection and catalog pages** with faceted filtering in a sidebar or a horizontal bar, sorting, numbered pages, a load more button or infinite scroll, a grid view switcher, promotional tiles in the grid and a collection hero.
- **Search** with predictive results, a featured collection, popular searches and recent searches before the shopper types, and a results page that switches between products, articles and pages.
- **Cart drawer or cart page**, opening on add to cart, with a free shipping bar, product suggestions, gift wrap, suggestions for an empty cart, order notes, a discount code field, selling plans, unit pricing, Shop Pay Installments and accelerated checkout buttons.
- **Currency codes** after prices, set separately for product pages, cards, cart lines and the cart total.
- **Blog and article templates** with three listing layouts, a tag filter, reading time, related articles, comments, pull quotes and date formats.
- **Alternate templates**: editorial, gift card and quick order product templates, a lookbook collection template, and about, accessibility, contact with stores, drop, editorial, FAQ, lookbook and size guide page templates.
- **Contact page template** with a configurable form, subject options and a contact details panel.
- **Gift card page** with a QR code, copy-to-clipboard, Apple Wallet and print support.
- **Password page** with a countdown, timezone selection, email signup and a background image.
- **404 page** with a featured collection.
- **Policy page** template for every Shopify policy.
- **A back link and breadcrumb** at the top of most pages, shortened with an ellipsis when a name is long.
- **Footer** with a wordmark, navigation, text, contact, email signup, Follow on Shop, image and store policy blocks, social icons, payment icons, country and language selectors, and columns that can collapse into an accordion on mobile.
- **Announcement bar** with rotate or marquee motion, a per-message countdown and a copyable discount code.
- **Ten languages.** The storefront and the theme editor ship in English, Spanish, French, Italian, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Portuguese (Portugal), Dutch, Swedish and Polish, with dates and plural forms in the shopper's language.
- **Right-to-left layout** for right-to-left languages, with drawers, carousels and arrows following the reading direction.
- **B2B** support: the company and location a buyer is ordering for in the header, volume pricing on prices and cards, tax-exclusive price notes and a quick order list section.
- **Accessibility work across the theme**: stop, previous and next controls on the rotating announcement bar, dialogs and drawers that move focus to their title and return it on close, respect for reduced motion throughout, and a keyboard and automated audit of every template and popup.
- **Motion settings**: page transitions, a card image that morphs into the product page image, a card hover effect and reveal on scroll, all skipped for shoppers who ask for reduced motion.
- **Structured data** for Product, Collection, Blog, Article, Organization and Breadcrumb, plus Open Graph and Twitter Card metadata on every page.
- **Multi-currency and multi-language** through Shopify Markets.
- **Pickup availability**, **Shop Pay Installments** and **app block support** across the main sections.
