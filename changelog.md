# Changelog

Pave version history. The current version is listed at the top.

## 1.0.0 — May 2026

The first public release of Pave on the Shopify Theme Store.

### Added

- Editorial side-panel navigation with multi-level menu support, predictive search, account, cart, and policy links.
- Three default color schemes (light, surface, dark) with a custom color scheme builder.
- Hero banner, New arrivals, Brand message, Collection list, Brand image, and Newsletter sections for the home page.
- Customer reviews, FAQ, and Rich text with image sections for content pages.
- Recently viewed and Product recommendations sections for the product and cart pages.
- Complementary products section powered by Shopify Search & Discovery.
- Featured product section with full app-block support.
- Custom Liquid section for advanced customization.
- Product page with sticky info, configurable gallery layouts (Stacked, 2 columns, Thumbnails, Thumbnail slider), and rich block library: Vendor, Title, Price, Variant picker, Buy buttons, Description, Collapsible tabs, Pop-up, Share, Product rating, Icon with text, Custom Liquid.
- Variant picker with color chip and image swatch support.
- Buy buttons with dynamic checkout and gift card recipient form.
- Collapsible tabs with translated default headings (Description, Composition and care, Shipping and returns) or custom.
- Cart page with discount code field, accelerated checkout buttons, selling plans, and unit pricing.
- Collection page with hero banner, faceted filtering, sort, and quick view.
- Catalog page with sidebar quick navigation.
- Search page with predictive search and empty-state featured collection.
- Blog and article templates with configurable card elements and pull quotes.
- Page and contact page templates, including a styled contact form section.
- 404 page with featured collection.
- Gift card page with QR code, copy-to-clipboard, Apple Wallet pass, and print support.
- Password page with countdown timer, email signup, and background image.
- Policy page template for Privacy, Terms, Refund, and Shipping policies.
- Footer with brand text, navigation columns, social icons, payment icons, Follow on Shop, country and language selectors.
- Announcement bar with optional link.
- Image focal points (desktop and mobile), variant images, and 3D model media support.
- JSON-LD structured data for Product, Collection, Blog, FAQ, Organization, and Breadcrumb.
- Open Graph and Twitter Card metadata on every page.
- Multi-currency and multi-language support via Shopify Markets.
- Pickup availability on the product page.
- Shop Pay Installments banner.
- Follow on Shop button.
- Compatibility with Shopify app blocks across all section groups.
- Compatibility with classic and new customer accounts.

### Known issues

The launch version is known to have a small number of issues that we are tracking and will resolve in subsequent patch releases. See [Contact](../support/contact/) to report any issue you encounter.
