# Changelog

Pave version history, newest first.

## 1.0.0

The first release of the theme.

Pave is built for stores that lead with photography: a spacious, editorial storefront where the images carry the page and the interface stays out of the way. Colour, type and product card styling are set once in theme settings and apply across every template, so a store can be rebranded without opening a section.

### Added

- **Four variant picker styles** — three swatch sizes and a dropdown for catalogs with many options — chosen once in theme settings and applied to every product page.
- **Side-panel navigation** with three levels of menu nesting, predictive search, the customer account component, the cart, a Follow on Shop button and your policy links.
- **Home page sections**: Hero banner, New arrivals, Brand message, Collection list, Brand image, Journal, Newsletter and Newsletter popup.
- **Content sections**: Rich text with image, Customer reviews and FAQ.
- **Merchandising sections**: Featured product with full app-block support, Product recommendations in related or complementary mode, and Recently viewed.
- **Custom Liquid** as both a section and a block across the main sections.
- **Product page** with a configurable gallery (Stacked, 2 columns, Thumbnails, Thumbnail slideshow), sticky product information, and blocks for vendor, heading, price, text, SKU, inventory status, variant picker, buy buttons, description, collapsible tabs, pop-up, share, product rating, icon with text and custom Liquid.
- **Size guide** from a page, per product via metafield, and optionally on every option.
- **Rich product media** including video, external video and 3D models, with image focal points for desktop and mobile.
- **Product cards** with quick add, colour swatches, a configurable image ratio and a new badge with a configurable window.
- **Discount display settings** governing badge wording, minimum threshold, colours, and promotion labels driven by product metafields.
- **Collection and catalog pages** with faceted filtering, sorting, a sidebar menu and a collection hero.
- **Search page** with the same filters as a collection, plus a featured collection for empty results.
- **Cart** with order notes, a discount code field, selling plans, unit pricing and accelerated checkout buttons.
- **Blog and article templates** with configurable card elements, comments, pull quotes and date formats.
- **Contact page template** with a configurable form, subject options and a contact details panel.
- **Gift card page** with a QR code, copy-to-clipboard, Apple Wallet and print support.
- **Password page** with a countdown, timezone selection, email signup and a background image.
- **404 page** with a featured collection.
- **Policy page** template for every Shopify policy.
- **Footer** with a wordmark, navigation, text and contact blocks, social icons, payment icons, and country and language selectors.
- **Announcement bar** with rotate or marquee motion, a per-message countdown and a copyable discount code.
- **A colour scheme system** covering backgrounds, text, borders, buttons and sale prices, applied per section, with two schemes set up out of the box.
- **Structured data** for Product, Collection, Blog, Organization and Breadcrumb, plus Open Graph and Twitter Card metadata on every page.
- **Multi-currency and multi-language** through Shopify Markets.
- **Pickup availability**, **Shop Pay Installments** and **app block support** across the main sections.
