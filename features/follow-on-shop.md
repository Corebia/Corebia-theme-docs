# Follow on Shop

The **Follow on Shop** button lets customers follow your store from the Shop app (Shopify's consumer mobile app). Followers can quickly find your store again, get notified of new products, and complete purchases with a single tap via Shop Pay.

## Where it appears

- The [Footer](../../sections/footer/) — when the **Show Follow on Shop** setting is enabled.

## Prerequisites

The Follow on Shop button works automatically when:

- The Shop app is enabled for your store (this is on by default for stores that accept Shop Pay).
- The footer setting **Show Follow on Shop** is enabled in Pave (the default).

You don't need any additional app installation; the button is rendered directly by Shopify.

## What customers see

A button with the Shop logo and the text "Follow on Shop" (or a localized variation depending on the customer's locale). Tapping the button:

- On a mobile device with the Shop app installed: opens the app and adds the store to the customer's followed list.
- On a mobile device without the Shop app: prompts to install the app, then follows the store after install.
- On desktop: opens the Shop web experience or prompts the customer to scan a QR code to install the app.

## Tips

- **Place the button in the footer.** That's the default. Adding it elsewhere is unnecessary — customers who reach the footer have engaged enough to be likely candidates to follow.
- **Don't disable it without reason.** Followers are a high-quality marketing channel: they've actively chosen to hear from you, and Shop Pay completes purchases in a single tap.
- **Combine with email signup.** Followers and email subscribers reach different audiences — one prefers app notifications, the other prefers email.

## Troubleshooting

- **The button doesn't appear** — Confirm **Show Follow on Shop** is enabled in the **Footer** section settings. If still missing, confirm Shopify Payments is set up, since Shop Pay is part of the Shopify Payments stack.
- **Tapping the button does nothing on desktop** — On desktop without the Shop app, tapping opens the Shop web app. Some customers will see a QR code prompt instead.
